# Article identity evidence for the 22 November 2026 sight review

The [full source-match report](sight-review-2026-evidence-report.md) and
[machine-readable record](sight-review-2026-remaining-evidence.json) are
**a frozen snapshot of the 1,568 sight records that still lacked individual
editorial decisions at the time this collection started**.

A live Wikipedia MediaWiki API search returned candidate encyclopedia pages.
Names were compared, with geographical distance where Wikipedia supplied GPS,
and country-context evidence. Ambiguous matches were not silently accepted.
A matched source only provides an external factual starting point: an
encyclopedia title alone does **not** establish visitor quality, global rarity,
a safe or open attraction, or the correctness of a score.

The 922 includes genuine source mismatches and lookup failures; neither implies
the attraction is invalid. The 598 are **candidate identity matches** rather
than 598 complete researched scoring decisions.

This report is a historical research snapshot: subsequent reviews of 70
additional sights reduce the **live unreviewed score backlog from 1,568 to
1,498**, but those newly reviewed records remain in this pre-review
source inventory to preserve provenance. The canonical per-sight review status
and before/after overall scores are in
[sight-review-2026-full-audit.md](sight-review-2026-full-audit.md).
