## DZ/sights/ighamaouen-granaries
Evidence status: source-linked-unverified for current departures and all six components. UNESCO tentative submission https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6829/ (opened, lines 83–95) lists six fortified collective granaries, including Aghem Draa at 29°32′5.16″N, 0°11′0.50″E. The Ksar Draa coordinates https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ksar_Draa match within rounding; https://virtualglobetrotting.com/map/ksar-draa-of-timimoun/ independently provides a close coordinate match. Znika https://www.znikaexperience.com/en/circuit-timimoun and Taan https://taan-ecotourism.com/timimoun/ advertise Ksar Draa excursions. Rate exterior heritage and desert setting at this component, not entry to unstable buildings or access to all six. US travel advice https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/algeria.html cautions against long-distance independent Sahara travel; Austria https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/algerien/ lists high risk for Timimoun reached by air. Organized trips do not establish safety. Exact excursion route and other components require separate security checks. Existing representative coordinate retained and not treated as a visitor entrance.

Impact 8.3: striking circular earthen fortress in dunes, limited standing architecture compared with leading fortified towns. Uniqueness 8.9: communal grain storage and defense in an arid landscape. Significance 8.8: historic vernacular construction and food security practices; tentative nomination, not inscription. Experience 7.5: advertised guided excursions, remote logistics and uncertain interior access. Depth 7.8: architectural and cultural interpretation of the series, only one verified named excursion destination. Comparisons: less extensive standing settlement than Ait Ben Haddou (Morocco), less intact communal storage interior than Ksar Ouled Soltane (Tunisia), less developed visitor interpretation than the M'Zab Valley (Algeria), less monumental than the fortified town of Shibam (Yemen). Category scores reflect documented architecture and cultural context, not tour availability. Overall 83.80.
