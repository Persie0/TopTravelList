# Nepal — initial inventory and quality audit

2026-11-25. 49 settlements and 123 sights: 172 individually named reviews, 114 source-linked-unverified and 58 editorial-only, zero fully fact-checked and zero unassessed. 42 records meet the 90+ or 9.5+ threshold and include written challenges. No perfect tens or national multipliers. All four [UNESCO World Heritage properties](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/NP) represented: Kathmandu Valley (all seven monument groups individually included), Lumbini, Chitwan and Sagarmatha. [Nepal Tourism Board destination inventory](https://ntb.gov.np/en/places-to-go), [protected areas](https://ntb.gov.np/en/protected-areas), [trekking](https://ntb.gov.np/en/things-to-do/trekking) and UNESCO search excerpts informed inventory and limited context. Links are not comprehensive site-by-site verification. Representative coordinates remain approximate and independently unchecked, especially routes and large parks. Peer ladders reuse current numerical ranking snapshots and sometimes mixed-type comparisons; four independent field-by-field comparator investigations were not completed for every record. Everest is Nepal-facing mountain landscape viewing, not summit climbing; the summit coordinate is a border representative point. Base camps, trails and gateway settlements scored separately. Wildlife ratings concern habitat and visitor experience, not guaranteed sightings. Trek scores assume ordinary authorized prepared visitor conditions, not a safety or access promise; [NTB TIMS guidance](https://ntb.gov.np/en/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/tims-card) should be checked before travel. Inventory not exhaustive; remote western and eastern villages, individual peaks and local heritage sites need further research. Existing country scores and rules unchanged.
