## Method

All 430 newly recorded destinations were judged individually under the **unchanged** v2 core weights: impact 30%, uniqueness 25%, significance 20%, experience 15%, depth 10%. Four international peers were named for each; individual scope and strength/weakness were described, and applicable categories were scored. City scores refer to the settlement itself, not the adjacent landmark. A large park or region has an approximate representative coordinate and no assumed entrance or current access guarantee. No world-best 10 registry approvals, country multipliers, country quotas or archived one-time migration curves were used.

Six **individual** rail/museum score revisions, with before/after values and distinct reasons, are in [secondary calibration](second-calibration-2026.md). Those current YAML values supersede first-pass review notes.

## Outstanding research

Independently validate identity, exact site scope, coordinates, historical/environmental claims, specific access and at least four meaningful global peer relationships **for each entry**. Attach dedicated links, publisher and access date. Recheck 90+ sites and any category 9.5+ against current world-class peers, without imposing a target distribution. This is substantial follow-up research; the initial India editorial inventory is not exhaustive.

Append any later review as a dated decision. Do not rewrite the historical 2026 global audit or these first-pass decisions.
