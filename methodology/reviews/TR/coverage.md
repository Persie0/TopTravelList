# Türkiye — initial heritage coverage

2026-11-25. [UNESCO national list](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/tr/) records 22 inscribed properties and 80 tentative nominations. All inscribed properties represented. This initial batch represents the following 15 tentative nominations:

- Sümela Monastery
- St. Nicholas Church
- Ishak Pasha Palace
- Kekova
- Güllük Dagi-Termessos National Park
- Archaeological Site of Sagalassos
- Archaeological Site of Perge
- Archeological Site of Zeugma
- Lake Tuz Special Environmental Protection Area
- Çanakkale and Gelibolu Battles Zones
- Akdamar Church
- The Theatre and Aqueducts of the Ancient City of Aspendos
- The Bodrum Castle
- Mardin Cultural Landscape
- Karain Cave

Remaining 65 tentative nominations need individual scope and duplicate checks using the national inventory. Some are represented partially by cities but are not counted as full nominated sights. This is not an exhaustive country inventory. Coordinates, condition, access and current comparison values require verification.
