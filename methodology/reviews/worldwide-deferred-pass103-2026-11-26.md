## sight-it-papal-villas — Villas of the Papal Nobility
- Evidence status: source-linked-unverified
- Tentative series includes Palazzo Farnese at Caprarola and Villa Lante, both visitor destinations with documented historic architecture and gardens. Other private estates may not be accessible.
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/351/ ; https://villemonumentalituscia.cultura.gov.it/ ; https://cultura.gov.it/luogo/villa-lante

## sight-es-la-palma-engravings — Rock engravings of La Palma
- Evidence status: source-linked-unverified
- Tentative series explicitly identifies publicly opened archaeological parks at La Zarza and Belmaco; protected stations elsewhere are not assumed accessible.
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6812/

## sight-es-mediterranean-rock-art — Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin
- Evidence status: source-linked-unverified
- Inscribed series. Spanish Ministry of Culture documents guided visits to specified shelters in Valltorta-Gasulla; Catalan heritage portal identifies additional visitable groups.
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/874 ; https://arampi.cultura.gob.es/cuevas-abrigos/comunitat-valenciana/conjuntos-arte-rupestre-visitable/valltorta-gasulla.html ; https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/en/visitable-sites

## sight-es-mozarabic-buildings — Mozarabic buildings
- Evidence status: source-linked-unverified
- Tentative group includes San Miguel de Escalada and Santiago de Peñalba; municipal tourism pages document their architecture and visits. Limited access and worship restrictions apply.
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6377/ ; https://www.aytogradefes.es/turismo-y-ocio/patrimonio/san-miguel-de-escalada.html ; https://www.ponferrada.org/turismo/es/monumentos/iglesia-santiago-penalba

## sight-es-frontier-forts — Bulwarked frontier fortifications
- Evidence status: source-linked-unverified
- Tentative proposal includes Ciudad Rodrigo, where the municipal tourism authority identifies the defensive walls as a visitor attraction. Other components require separate access checks.
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/1021/ ; https://turismo.ciudadrodrigo.es/
