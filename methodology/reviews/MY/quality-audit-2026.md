Inventory uses [Malaysia's government tourism directory](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/categories/tourism/domestic-travel/tourist-destinations-in-malaysia), [Tourism Malaysia](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore), [official tourism brochures](https://ebrochures.tourismmalaysia.app/), the [UNESCO country list](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/my), the [Sarawak Forestry Department national park list](https://forestry.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/webpage_view/1012), and regional tourism references. These support inventory discovery, not every specific assertion.

All six inscribed World Heritage properties are represented: Gunung Mulu, Kinabalu, Lenggong archaeological heritage, both Melaka and George Town components, Niah cave archaeological heritage, and FRIM Forest Park (2025). Taman Negara, Gombak Selangor Quartz Ridge and Sungai Buloh Leprosarium are tentative candidates, not inscribed properties. All locations are approximate representative coordinates, not verified entrances. Layang Layang is administered by Malaysia but lies in a disputed maritime area; inclusion is a travel organization convention, not a sovereignty claim or recommendation to visit. Check access and government travel advisories before travel, especially for eastern Sabah, remote protected areas, sensitive communities and offshore destinations.

The five core fields are independently reasoned per record and benchmarked using numerical peer groups from the existing worldwide ranking pages. Groups are reused by attraction type; some mixes are imperfect (garden/park/theme park, cave/geological/cultural comparisons, market/urban street). They are provisional ladders, not a complete independently researched field-by-field four-peer analysis. Category ratings are editorial. No exact 10, global weights change, country multiplier or historical recalibration script. The [high-score review](high-score-review-2026-11-25.md) challenges all 90+ totals; 9.5+ fields are separately discussed in initial decisions. Inventory is not exhaustive, especially smaller regional destinations. Exact-site claims and GPS remain the primary research backlog.
