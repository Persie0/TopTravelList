# IO — Chagos Archipelago / British Indian Ocean Territory worldwide V2 reviews

The existing ISO code IO is retained for geographic indexing, not a determination of sovereignty. [House of Commons Library briefing](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10512/) explains the 2019 ICJ advisory opinion, the 2025 UK–Mauritius agreement and the implementing bill that fell in 2026; treaty implementation is not assumed complete. [Mauritian government geography](https://chagosarchipelago.govmu.org/Pages/Location_and_History.aspx) presents its sovereignty position. The history of forced displacement of Chagossians is integral to this coverage; see [House of Lords report](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/ld5901/ldselect/ldintrel/234/23405.htm). The [12 November 2026 appeal judgment](https://www.bailii.org/io/cases/BIOT/2026/CA1.html) records the February 2026 return and encampment on Île du Coin. This is not an established ordinary tourist settlement and no inference about the legitimacy of resettlement is intended. 

[Current administration travel advice](https://www.biot.gov.io/visiting/current-travel-advice/) explicitly excludes ordinary tourism; yacht permits are for safe passage, and Diego Garcia access is restricted to authorized official duty. No visitor impact, experience or depth score assigned. Research on [microplastic contamination](https://conbio.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/csp2.70059) cautions against assuming pristine reefs; no current site-level coral or wildlife census is implied. Access and legal status require renewed review after changes. All sources consulted 2026-11-26; government access, UK parliamentary briefing, Mauritian geography and research article opened; other sources inspected through search excerpts.
