# IO — settlement coverage

No established ordinary visitor settlement records. Military installations are not city attractions. The [November 2026 judgment](https://www.bailii.org/io/cases/BIOT/2026/CA1.html) documents the Chagossian return to Île du Coin; no city rating is assigned to the encampment. This does not deny historic habitation or Chagossian rights. Revisit if resettlement develops and visitor scope is documented.
