## Follow-up on existing ranked records

The Ghana Museums and Monuments Board closure notice also covers currently ranked Fort Apollonia, Fort St. Anthony and Ussher Fort. Ussher and James were subsequently reopened under controlled conditions; no confirmed reopening for Apollonia or St Anthony was found in this pass. Their experience and depth fields should receive a targeted reassessment rather than silently assuming full access or removing scores without reviewing their specific scope. See https://gmmb.gov.gh/2426-2/ and https://www.myjoyonline.com/government-reopens-ussher-and-james-forts-after-closure-imposes-strict-new-rules/. These three are flagged follow-ups, not counted among the 100 unassessed records or four completions.
