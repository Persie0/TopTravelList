Reopen only for concrete new evidence of a meaningful gap. Never add filler, duplicate a parent experience, lower the inclusion bar to meet a quota, or keep searching a country merely to fill a pass. Move to another country once the high-value queue is exhausted. A country may be paused rather than incorrectly marked done if known candidates remain.
